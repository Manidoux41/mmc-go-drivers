### 2. Long Description (Max 4000 characters)

**MMC Go Drivers: The ultimate assistant for transport professionals.**

Whether you are a bus driver, taxi driver, truck driver, or fleet manager, MMC Go Drivers was designed to simplify your daily life, secure your trips, and optimize your organization.

**Why choose MMC Go Drivers?**

📍 **SPECIALIZED & SMART NAVIGATION**
*   **Truck (HGV) Routing**: Calculate your trips based on your vehicle's dimensions (height, width, weight) to avoid low bridges or restricted roads.
*   **Turn-by-Turn Guidance**: Enjoy clear directional instructions with remaining distance and real-time arrival time.
*   **Custom Layers**: Switch between Map, high-resolution Satellite, and Relief (Terrain) views for a perfect reading of the landscape.
*   **Anti-Sleep**: The screen stays on while you navigate or record, ensuring uninterrupted GPS tracking.

📊 **RECORDING & OPTIMIZATION (The "Magic Wand" 🪄 effect)**
*   **Trip Memory**: Record your lines and circuits including all your stops (waypoints).
*   **Unique Optimization**: Transform your raw GPS traces into perfect road-following paths with our smart tool.
*   **KML Import/Export**: Import your own KML files (from WhatsApp, Email, or Google Earth) or export your trips to share them.

🗓️ **PLANNING & COMPLIANCE**
*   **Mission Management**: View your daily planning in a fluid and intuitive way.
*   **Regulatory Alerts**: Receive automatic notifications to respect driving, break, and service time (European Social Regulations).
*   **Collective Ticket (BC)**: Specific management for tourist and school transport.

📁 **FLEET & DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT**
*   **Digital Wallet**: Keep your licenses, registration cards, and insurance certificates always at hand.
*   **Admin Console (Diamond Option)**: Create accounts for your drivers, manage the entire company's planning, and generate weekly PDF reports.

🌍 **AN INTERNATIONAL APPLICATION**
Fully translated into 8 languages to support all drivers: French, English, German, Spanish, Chinese, Japanese, Khmer, and Arabic (Full RTL support).
